# MWECAU DigiVote

**Focus:** University voting platform  
**Repository:** [mwecau_digivote_laravel](https://github.com/johncharlessarwi-dot/mwecau_digivote_laravel)  
**Visibility:** public

## Problem being explored

Student elections require clear identity checks, election eligibility rules and protected vote recording.

## Engineering scope

Laravel-based election flows, registration-number access, election levels and administrative reporting.

## What a reviewer should examine

Look for separation of voter identity from ballot content, duplicate-vote prevention, and independent audit trails.

## Status and access notes

A software showcase cannot establish legal or institutional election authorization.

For implementation details, see the linked repository documentation when you have access. This summary avoids claiming production readiness beyond published evidence.
