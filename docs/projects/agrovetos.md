# Bhoke AgrovetOS

**Focus:** Agrovet inventory and ordering  
**Repository:** [bhoke-agrovert-sytem](https://github.com/johncharlessarwi-dot/bhoke-agrovert-sytem)  
**Visibility:** private

## Problem being explored

Agricultural and veterinary retailers must coordinate stock, prices, expiry-sensitive items and customer requests.

## Engineering scope

Product catalogue, pricing, inventory tracking, expiry information and order-oriented business workflows.

## What a reviewer should examine

Evaluate quantity consistency, product safety classifications, user access and clear separation of enquiries from confirmed orders.

The code repository is private; a linked live demo may have different availability or capabilities.
